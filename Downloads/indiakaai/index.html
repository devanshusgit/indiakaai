Admin Guide — IndiaKaAI

How to Sign In as Admin

1. Click Sign In in the top nav
2. Use your registered admin email
3. Enter your password
4. You'll see the Admin badge appear in the navbar
5. Click it or go to Blog → Manage Posts

How to Add a Blog Post

1. Go to Admin → Blog Posts tab
2. Click Add New Post
3. Fill in: Title, Category, Emoji, Excerpt, Source URL, Author, Read Time
4. Click Save Post — it appears live on the Blog page instantly
5. To edit or delete, click or on any post card

How to Add an AI Tool

1. Go to Admin → AI Tools tab
2. Click Add New Tool
3. Fill in: Tool Name, Website URL (logo auto-loads!), Category, Description, Pricing
4. Click Save Tool — it appears live on the Home page immediately
5. To edit or delete, click or directly on any tool card (visible only to you)

1. Who We Are

IndiaKaAI is an independent AI tools directory operated in India. We curate and list AI tools to help individuals and businesses discover the right software for their needs. We are not a marketplace and do not facilitate transactions between users and tool providers.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate this service:

  • Account data: Name and email address, collected when you voluntarily create an account (Sign Up).
  • Contact form data: Name, email and message content submitted via our Contact Us, Submit a Tool, or Get In Touch forms.
  • Newsletter data: Email address submitted voluntarily for our weekly newsletter.
  • Browser storage: We use your browser's localStorage to store your account session and preferences. No cookies are set by IndiaKaAI itself.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account on this platform.
  • To respond to enquiries, tool submissions and collaboration requests sent via our contact forms.

  • To improve the directory and add new tools based on submissions.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. The only third-party service we use is EmailJS (emailjs.com) solely to route contact form submissions to our inbox. EmailJS processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy. No other third parties receive your data.

5. Data Storage & Security

Account data (name, email, hashed password) is stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device — we do not maintain a centralised user database. Contact form data is transmitted via EmailJS and stored in our email inbox. We take reasonable precautions to protect this data but cannot guarantee absolute security over email.

6. Your Rights under the DPDP Act 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a summary of personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data (account data can be cleared from your browser's localStorage directly; for form/email data, contact us).
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe from our newsletter or stop using our service at any time.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India if your rights are not honoured.

To exercise any of these rights, use the Contact Us form on this site.

This service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
