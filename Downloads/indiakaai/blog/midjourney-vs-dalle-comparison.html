Midjourney vs DALL-E 3: Which AI Image Generator to Choose?

Published on April 27, 2026 • 8 min read

AI image generation has revolutionized digital art and design. Midjourney and DALL-E 3 are the two leading AI image generators in 2026, each with unique strengths. This comprehensive comparison will help you choose the right tool for your needs.

Quick Comparison Table

Feature Midjourney DALL-E 3
Pricing $10/month (~₹830) Free (Bing) or ₹1,650/month (ChatGPT Plus)

Midjourney: The Artistic Powerhouse

Midjourney is known for creating stunning, artistic images that often look like professional artwork or photography.

Pros of Midjourney

  • Exceptional image quality: Creates breathtaking, artistic images
  • Consistent style: Maintains a cohesive aesthetic across generations
  • Advanced parameters: Fine-tune aspect ratios, styles, and more
  • Active community: Large Discord community for inspiration
  • Regular updates: Frequent improvements and new features

Cons of Midjourney

  • Requires Discord: Must use Discord app, which can be confusing
  • No free tier: Minimum $10/month subscription required
  • Poor text rendering: Struggles with text in images
  • Learning curve: Takes time to master prompt engineering
  • Public by default: Your images are visible to others unless you pay for private mode

Best Use Cases for Midjourney

  • Concept art and illustrations
  • Fantasy and sci-fi artwork
  • Book covers and album art
  • Artistic portraits and landscapes
  • Social media graphics with artistic flair

Pricing in India: Midjourney Basic plan costs $10/month (~₹830). Standard plan is $30/month (~₹2,490) with more generations.

DALL-E 3: The Versatile Generator

DALL-E 3 by OpenAI excels at understanding complex prompts and creating photorealistic images with accurate text rendering.

Pros of DALL-E 3

  • Free option available: Use via Bing Image Creator for free
  • Excellent prompt understanding: Interprets complex descriptions accurately
  • Perfect text rendering: Can add text to images correctly
  • Easy to use: Simple web interface, no Discord needed
  • ChatGPT integration: Generate images while chatting
  • Photorealistic results: Great for realistic images

Cons of DALL-E 3

  • Less artistic: Images can look more generic compared to Midjourney
  • Limited style control: Fewer parameters to customize output
  • Slower on free tier: Bing version has generation limits
  • Content policy: Stricter restrictions on what you can generate

Best Use Cases for DALL-E 3

  • Product mockups and designs
  • Marketing materials with text
  • Infographics and educational content
  • Realistic photos and scenes
  • Quick prototyping and ideation

Pricing in India: DALL-E 3 is free via Bing Image Creator. For unlimited access via ChatGPT, you need ChatGPT Plus at ₹1,650/month.

Image Quality Comparison

Both tools produce high-quality images, but with different strengths:

Midjourney Excels At:

  • Artistic and stylized images
  • Fantasy and imaginative scenes
  • Consistent aesthetic across images
  • Dramatic lighting and composition

DALL-E 3 Excels At:

  • Photorealistic images
  • Following complex instructions
  • Adding accurate text to images
  • Diverse styles and subjects

Ease of Use

Winner: DALL-E 3

DALL-E 3 is significantly easier to use:

  • DALL-E 3: Simple web interface (Bing or ChatGPT). Type prompt, get image.
  • Midjourney: Requires Discord account, learning slash commands, and understanding parameters.

Pricing: Which is Better Value?

For Budget Users: DALL-E 3 via Bing Image Creator (Free)

For Serious Creators: Midjourney Basic ($10/month) offers better value than ChatGPT Plus ($20/month)

Cost Breakdown for India:

  • Free option: DALL-E 3 via Bing (limited generations)
  • Budget option: Midjourney Basic at ₹830/month
  • Premium option: ChatGPT Plus at ₹1,650/month (includes DALL-E 3 + GPT-4)

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Midjourney If:

  • You want the most artistic, stunning images
  • You're creating concept art, illustrations, or fantasy artwork
  • You don't mind using Discord
  • You're willing to invest time learning prompt engineering
  • You need consistent artistic style

Choose DALL-E 3 If:

  • You want a free option to start
  • You need text in your images
  • You prefer a simple, easy-to-use interface
  • You want photorealistic images
  • You already use ChatGPT and want image generation too

Use Both If:

  • You're a professional designer or artist
  • You need different styles for different projects
  • Budget allows for both subscriptions

Our Recommendation for Indian Users: Start with DALL-E 3 via Bing (free) to learn AI image generation. If you love it and need better quality, upgrade to Midjourney Basic at ₹830/month.

Alternatives to Consider

If neither Midjourney nor DALL-E 3 fits your needs, consider:

  • Stable Diffusion: Free, open-source, run locally
  • Leonardo AI: Free tier available, good for game assets
  • Canva AI: Built into Canva, great for quick designs
  • Adobe Firefly: Integrated with Adobe Creative Cloud

Conclusion

Both Midjourney and DALL-E 3 are excellent AI image generators with different strengths:

  • Midjourney is the best choice for artistic, stunning images and serious creators
  • DALL-E 3 is perfect for beginners, budget users, and those needing text in images

The good news? You can try DALL-E 3 for free via Bing Image Creator right now. If you want to level up, Midjourney's $10/month plan is affordable and delivers exceptional results.
